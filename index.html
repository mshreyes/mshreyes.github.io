Senior Research Fellow • PhD Scholar

Shreyes Madgaonkar

Senior Research Fellow and PhD Scholar in the Computational Biology Group at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai, India.

My research lies at the intersection of Exposome Science, One Health, Computational Toxicology, and Environmental Risk Assessment. I use computational modeling, network science, and data-driven approaches to investigate the biological and ecological impacts of various environmental exposures across humans, animals, and ecosystems.

Member of Society of Environmental Toxicology and Chemistry (SETAC)

Research

Research Themes

I develop computational approaches to understand how environmental exposures influence biological systems across molecular, organismal, and ecosystem scales.

01

Exposome Science

Understanding how chemical, biological, and environmental exposures interact across the lifespan and influence health outcomes.

Toxicology · Exposure Biology · Aggregate Exposure Pathways
02

One Health

Studying interactions between human, animal, and ecosystem health through computational and ecological frameworks.

Ecology · Networks · Epidemiology
03

Computational Toxicology

Developing network toxicology frameworks such as Adverse Outcome Pathways (AOPs), integrating machine learning approaches to characterize chemical hazards and elucidate mechanisms of toxicity.

Adverse Outcome Pathways · Risk Assessment · Machine Learning
04

Toxicological Knowledgebases

Building FAIR resources that connect chemical, biological, and ecological information for reproducible research.

FAIR Data · Ontologies · Data Integration

Resources

Knowledgebases & Resources

I have contributed to the development of openly accessible resources that support research in environmental health, computational biology, and One Health.

ReCAnt

ReCAnt is a curated knowledgebase of chemicals used in aquaculture integrating chemical properties, ecotoxicity, environmental occurrence, regulations, and predicted biotransformation products.

Visit Resource →

TickMapKB

TickMapKB is a spatial knowledgebase of Indian ticks integrating species distributions, host associations, acaricide resistance, proteins, and geospatial records to support One Health research.

Visit Resource →

Heavy Metal-specific Toxicity Networks

A network-based resource integrating Aggregate Exposure Pathways (AEPs), Adverse Outcome Pathways (AOPs), and stressor-species networks to investigate the impacts of inorganic arsenic and cadmium on human and ecosystem health.

Visit Resource →

Stressor-AOP Network for Plastic Additives

A knowledgebase linking plastic additives with Adverse Outcome Pathways (AOPs) to support mechanistic understanding of chemical toxicity and risk assessment across biological systems.

Visit Resource →
